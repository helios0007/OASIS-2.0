            Single-zone EnergyPlus 26.2 model, Chennai EPW. A safe hour has an indoor heat index (US NWS) below 41 °C, with the ceiling fan's ASHRAE 55 cooling effect (about 2 °C, worked out every hour) except during the power cut. Children are covered by the heat index; JOS-3 is an adult model. Calibrated on 12 measured Dhaka homes (hourly error about 1.1 °C) and checked on Delhi; no Chennai homes yet, and household, routine and glass are assumptions.
